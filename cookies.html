What cookies are

A cookie is a small piece of text a website saves in your browser. “Local storage” is a similar feature that keeps data in your browser for a website. This page covers both.

The cookie we set

NameWhat it is forHow long it lasts
kepler_deviceA random ID that recognises this browser, so the site owner can see which devices have used Kepler AI, when, and from roughly where (city level, worked out from your IP address). It contains no name, email or password.1 year, renewed each visit

This is a first-party cookie: only Kepler AI sets and reads it. It is sent with your messages to our server (on Supabase), which records the device type, sign-ins, message count and approximate location. See the Privacy Policy for what is recorded and who can see it.

Local storage we use

NameWhat it is forHow long it lasts
sb-…-auth-tokenYour sign-in, so you stay signed in. Not saved at all if you choose “this visit only”.Until the “stay signed in” time you chose runs out
kepler-untilWhen your chosen “stay signed in” time endsUntil that time, or until you lock the site
kepler-stayThe “stay signed in” option you last pickedUntil you clear your browser data
kepler-model, kepler-effortThe AI model and effort level you last pickedUntil you clear your browser data

Third parties

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. When a reply contains a picture, your browser loads it from Pollinations (image.pollinations.ai), and the code library supabase-js loads from jsDelivr. These services receive your IP address and browser details as part of delivering the file, under their own policies. Neither sets cookies through Kepler AI.

Your choices

  • You can delete the cookie and local storage at any time in your browser’s settings (look for “site data” or “cookies” for this site). Kepler AI then treats your browser as a new device.
  • You can block cookies for this site in your browser. The chat still works, but a new device ID is made on each visit.
  • To have the record for your device deleted, contact [contact email].
