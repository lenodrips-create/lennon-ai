Who we are

Kepler AI is a chat website run by [legal name of the business or person], trading as Kepler Designs (“we”). Contact us about privacy at [contact email].

What we collect

When you create an account or sign in

  • Email and password. If you sign up with email, we collect your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase. We cannot see your password.
  • GitHub sign-in. If you choose “Continue with GitHub”, GitHub shares your account details with us, which can include your name, GitHub username, email address and profile picture link.
  • Guest use. If you choose “Skip for now”, we create an anonymous guest account. It has an ID but no email address.

When you use Kepler AI

  • Your chats. The messages you send, the replies you receive and a title made from your first message. Text from any file you attach becomes part of your message.
  • Profile settings. The display name you choose, your status (online, focus or offline) and your light or dark setting.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, the services that deliver this site receive your IP address and browser details when your browser contacts them (see “Services we use” below).

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To generate replies: each time you send a message, your recent conversation (up to 30 messages) is sent to Groq, which runs the AI models.
  • To save your chats and settings so you can see them again.

Storage on your device

We do not set cookies. We use your browser’s local storage for things the site needs to work:

  • your sign-in session (kept until you sign out; if you untick “Keep me signed in” it is kept only until you close the tab)
  • whether you asked to stay signed in
  • the AI model and effort level you last picked

How long we keep it

Chats are kept until you delete them with “Clear all chats” in the menu. Your account and profile are kept until you ask us to delete them. [Owner to decide: a retention period for inactive accounts and guest accounts]. Our service providers may keep logs for their own periods.
