What we collect

When you sign in

  • No account or email. Kepler AI has one shared password. Everyone who knows it signs in to the same site account, so chats are visible to everyone with the password.
  • Your device. We set a cookie with a random device ID (see the Cookie Policy). With it we record your browser and device type (for example “iPhone · Safari”), when the device first and last used Kepler AI, and how many times it signed in and sent messages.
  • Approximate location. When you sign in or send a message, we look up your IP address to find an approximate location: city, region, country, time zone, rough coordinates and internet provider. We keep only those results and a scrambled (hashed) copy of the IP address, not the IP address itself.

When you use Kepler AI

  • Your chats. The messages you send, the replies you receive and a title made from your first message. Text from any file you attach becomes part of your message.
  • Profile settings. The display name you choose, your status (online, focus or offline) and your light or dark setting.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, the services that deliver this site receive your IP address and browser details when your browser contacts them (see “Services we use” below).

The site owner can see the device list and locations on a password-protected admin page. We do not use advertising tools, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To generate replies: each time you send a message, your recent conversation (up to 60 messages) is sent to Groq, which runs the AI models.
  • To save your chats and settings so you can see them again.

How long we keep it

Chats are kept until they are deleted (for example with the /wipe command). Device records and approximate locations are kept until the owner deletes them.
